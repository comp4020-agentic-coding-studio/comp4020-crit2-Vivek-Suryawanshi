Canberra Refugee Support

Canberra Refugee Support (CRS) is a Canberra not-for-profit run entirely by volunteers. It matches small teams of volunteers with asylum seekers and refugees who are registered with the organisation, offering longer-term, practical support — things like housing, furniture, English language training and finding services people are already entitled to. This is a student redesign concept, not CRS's real site; for the organisation itself, visit canberrarefugee.org.au.

Learn more at canberrarefugee.org.au.
