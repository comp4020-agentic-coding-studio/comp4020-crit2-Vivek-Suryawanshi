Volunteer roles

No prior experience or skills are required — volunteers are supported by the CRS committee and by volunteer support coordinators. Roles include:

  • Joining a good neighbour support team for a particular family or individual.
  • One-off requests: house moves, job search, household items, transport to appointments, garden working bees, sport, or tutoring.
  • Fundraising.
  • The scholarship program.
  • Administrative tasks and projects.
  • The CRS committee (governance).

How to volunteer

Becoming a member is the first step of volunteering, not a separate thing you do beforehand — it starts the same path that ends with being matched to a role.

  1. Become a CRS member.
  2. Attend an orientation session (before or after joining).
  3. Apply for a Working with Vulnerable People (WWVP) card for an active support role — free when it's for volunteer work only.
  4. Get matched: watch for volunteer requests emailed to members, or email CRS directly.
